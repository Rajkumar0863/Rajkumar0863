## 📂 Featured Consulting & Analytical Engagements

### 01 — 🏢 ConsultLab: End-to-End Business Analysis & Technology Consulting
**[Recommendation Deck (PDF) →](https://github.com/Rajkumar0863/consultlab/blob/main/05-deliverables/recommendation-deck.pdf) · [Repository →](https://github.com/Rajkumar0863/consultlab)**

A comprehensive consulting engagement applying **process mining and structured business analysis** to the BPI Challenge loan-application event log—reconstructing as-is operational reality across **1.2M events, 31,509 applications, and 149 staff**.

* **Key Findings:**
  * **33.1% post-offer drop-off:** 33.1% of applications cancel after formal offer issuance; post-offer follow-up accounts for **65.5% of total queue time** (40,321 waiting days vs. 181 handling days).
  * **Incompleteness Loop:** **73.4%** of successful applications enter document-incompleteness loops, adding ~5.6 days per case.
* **Consulting Recommendation:** Staged delivery of automated reminders and lapse-risk queue reprioritization; breaks even at a **2.4% recovery rate** (exposing €125,000 in Stage 1 to test assumptions at low cost).
* **Key Artifacts:** Project Brief, Stakeholder RACI, As-Is/To-Be BPMN, Requirements Traceability Matrix, Business Case with Sensitivity Analysis.

`Business Analysis` · `Process Mining` · `BPMN` · `Requirements Engineering` · `BABOK v3` · `Agile` · `Digital Transformation`
