### 📂 Featured Consulting & Analytical Projects

#### 01. ConsultLab — Business Analysis & Technology Consulting Engagement
* **The Business Problem:** Where does a B2B loan application process lose value, and what is the optimal digital transformation roadmap?
* **Analytical Methods:** Process Mining (BPI Challenge 1.2M event log), BPMN, BABOK v3, Sensitivity Analysis.
* **Key Findings:**
  * **33.1%** of applications cancel post-offer; **65.5%** of queue time (40,321 days) occurs during follow-up.
  * **73.4%** of successful applications pass through a document-incompleteness loop, adding 5.6 days each.
* **Consulting Recommendation:** Staged delivery of automated reminders and lapse-risk queue reprioritization; breaks even at a **2.4% recovery rate**.
* **Deliverables:** [Recommendation Deck (PDF)](./ConsultLab-Deck.pdf) | [BRD & Traceability Matrix](./ConsultLab)

#### 02. B2B Sales Pipeline & Revenue Leakage Analysis
* **The Business Problem:** Where is revenue leaking across 8,800 sales opportunities, and what is the recoverable value?
* **Tools & Tech:** `SQL`, `Python (pandas)`, `Tableau`, Commercial Analytics.
* **Key Findings:** Identified agent win-rate variance (55%–70%) and quantified a conservative **~$237K recoverable opportunity** by bringing below-median agents to team median performance.

#### 03. Retail Customer Growth & Retention Engine
* **The Business Problem:** Which customer segments generate sustainable value, and where is churn risk highest across 700K+ transactions?
* **Tools & Tech:** `Python`, `RFM Segmentation`, `Power BI`.
* **Key Findings:** Champions represent 34.8% of customers but **75.4% of revenue**; quantified a **£144K base-case retention opportunity**.
